Current Research Areas

Six Funded Research Programs

Every course includes at least 4 projects drawn from these active R&D efforts funded by NASA, NSF, ONR/US Navy, and other organizations.

🚁

Autonomous UAV, Mobile Vehicles & Aerial/Mobile Manipulation

Machine learning, deep learning, and reinforcement learning applied to aerial and mobile robotics control, navigation, and manipulation tasks. Platforms include drones, self-driving car modules, and traditional mobile robots.

👁️

Vision-Guided Object Detection & Tracking

Industrial manipulation in cluttered environments using computer vision and deep learning. Includes real-time detection, tracking, and semantic segmentation for robotic arms and autonomous systems.

🧬

AI-Assisted Genomic Analysis

Deep learning pipelines for genomic data processing, pattern recognition, and biomedical insight extraction. Includes classification, clustering, and sequence modeling on real biological datasets.

🧠

Disease Detection & Prevention

ML and deep learning for cancer, heart attack, brain tumor, Alzheimer's disease, and diabetes prediction as well as drug efficacy modeling. Uses public clinical and imaging datasets.

🌫️

Air Quality Monitoring & Warning

AI-driven systems for measuring, monitoring, predicting, and alerting on air quality conditions — including PM2.5, ozone, NO₂, and greenhouse gas concentration forecasting.

⛈️

Climate & Flood Prediction Systems

Greenhouse gas monitoring, precipitation analysis, and flash flood prediction and early warning systems. Uses NASA GPM, AERONET, PANDORA instruments and satellite data.
