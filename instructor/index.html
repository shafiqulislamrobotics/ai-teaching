About the Instructor

Research-driven teaching across four continents

Dr. Islam's research focuses on artificial intelligence, adaptive robotics and mechatronics, autonomous systems, computational biology, and climate computing. With over 28 years of teaching and curriculum development experience, he has contributed to programs in robotics, mechatronics, electrical and electronics engineering, and computer science, fostering innovation in intelligent and autonomous technologies.

Dr. Islam has secured research funding from numerous national and international organizations throughout his distinguished academic career spanning more than 28 years. He has played a pivotal role in advancing engineering education as a founding member of several Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering programs both internationally and in the United States. Most recently, he contributed to the development and delivery of the Bachelor of Science curriculum in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering at Xavier University of Louisiana.

In addition to his teaching and research accomplishments, Dr. Islam has served the scholarly community for over two decades as an editor, associate editor, guest editor, and editorial board member for numerous peer-reviewed journals and international conferences. His leadership and expertise continue to support innovation and excellence in robotics, autonomous systems, artificial intelligence, and interdisciplinary engineering research.
